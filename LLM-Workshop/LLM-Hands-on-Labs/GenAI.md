# Generative AI shortcomings 

1. Hallucinations 
Why it happens: The AI predicts words that “sound right” instead of fact-checking.

2. Knowledge Attribution 
Why it matters: Without knowing the source, you can’t fully trust or verify the answer. 

3. Knowledge Cutoff 
Why it matters: You may get outdated answers. 

4. Context Window Size

#### Meaning: The AI can only “remember” and process a limited amount of text in a conversation at once.

#### Example: If the context window is 10,000 words, and your chat goes beyond that, the AI may “forget” earlier parts of the conversation.

#### Why it matters: In long chats, you might need to remind the AI of important details.
