AI Applications’ Conversation agents category includes two app types: 
1. Conversational Agents 
2. Chat apps.

Selecting the Conversational Agents app type will take you directly to the Conversational Agents console, where you can create a hybrid conversational agent using a mix of Playbooks and Flows, enhanced by various Tools and Data Stores.

Selecting the Chat app type will take you through a short app creation process to deploy a Flow (formerly called a Dialogflow CX agent) with a single data store connected.

The Recommendations category in AI Applications includes three app types: Recommendation engine, Media recommendations, and Retail recommendations.
