#### Classification 
means that we assign an object to a specific category. The result you’re looking for can have one of several states. Examples of this type include the following:

- Recognition of dogs and cats in pictures (categories “dog” and “cat”)

- Evaluation in quality control (categories “good” and “bad”)

#### Regression 
is a method that can be used to predict specific numerical values. The result is a constant value. Examples include the following:

- Predicting the purchase price for a specific product

- Determining the monthly earnings of potential customers
